# Privacy Policy — Speedo

*Effective date: [fill in before publishing]*

Speedo ("the app") is developed by Future App Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

## Location data
Speedo uses your device's GPS to calculate and display your current speed, both in the app and in the Dynamic Island / Lock Screen Live Activity. Location data is:
- Used only on-device, in real time, to compute speed, distance, and trip statistics.
- **Never stored** beyond the current session (closing the app or resetting your trip clears it).
- **Never transmitted** to us or to any third party.
- Not linked to your identity.

You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Speedo**. Without location access, Speedo cannot display your speed.

## Advertising
Speedo is ad-supported and uses Google's AdMob SDK to show a banner ad and occasional interstitial ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising Identifier / IDFA) to serve ads and, if you consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, to personalize them. We do not control or have access to the data AdMob collects — see [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

If you decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt ("Ask App Not to Track"), Speedo continues to work exactly the same, and ads shown will not be personalized based on your activity in other apps.

## What we don't do
- No account or sign-up — the app has no backend.
- No first-party analytics SDK.
- No sale of personal data.
- No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Children's privacy
Speedo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above.
